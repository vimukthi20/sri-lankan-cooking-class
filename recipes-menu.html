Traditional Recipes & Feast Menus

Every dish taught in our cooking classes can be replicated in your own kitchen back home. Use our interactive dietary filter below to browse signature island dishes and cooking secrets.

🌶️🌶️ Medium Heat

Sri Lankan Village Chicken Curry

කුකුල් මස් ව්‍යංජනය (Kukul Mas Curry)

Tender chicken pieces simmered in deep roasted Ceylon curry powder, tempered mustard seeds, fresh pandan leaves, and thick second-press coconut milk.

Key Ingredients:
Ceylon Cinnamon Curry Leaves Roasted Curry Powder Coconut Milk Lemongrass
🌶️ Mild & Comforting
⏱️ Cook Time: 25 mins 100% Vegan & Gluten-Free

Golden Tempered Red Lentil Dhal

පරිප්පු හොද්ද (Parippu Kirata)

The comforting heartbeat of every Sri Lankan home. Red lentils cooked with turmeric and coconut milk, topped with fragrant sizzling garlic and curry leaves.

Key Ingredients:
Red Split Lentils Coconut Cream Wild Turmeric Mustard Seeds Garlic Slices
🌶️🌶️🌶️ High Heat (Adjustable)
⏱️ Prep Time: 15 mins Stone-Ground Specialty

Ancestral Granite-Ground Pol Sambol

පොල් සම්බෝල (Miris Gala Pol Sambol)

Freshly scraped coconut pounded on the granite slab with sun-dried red chili, red shallots, sea salt, black pepper, and freshly squeezed lime juice.

Key Ingredients:
Fresh Coconut Whole Dry Chili Shallots Lime Juice Black Pepper
🌶️ Mild (Served with Spicy Sambol)
⏱️ Cook Time: 20 mins Street Food Legend

Lace Bowl Egg Hoppers (Appa)

බිත්තර ආප්ප (Biththara Appa)

Crisp, paper-thin golden lace edges bowl with a soft, pillowy coconut center crowned by a gently steamed farm egg and freshly cracked black pepper.

Key Ingredients:
Fermented Rice Flour Coconut Toddy / Water Farm Fresh Eggs Coarse Black Pepper
🌶️🌶️ Rich & Earthy
⏱️ Cook Time: 90 mins 100% Plant-Based Meat

Slow-Cooked Young Jackfruit (Polos)

පොලොස් ඇඹුල (Polos Ambula)

Baby green jackfruit simmered for hours in clay pots with smoky dried goraka and dark roasted spices until the texture resembles tender slow-braised pulled beef.

Key Ingredients:
Young Baby Jackfruit Dried Goraka Dark Roasted Spices Thick Coconut Cream
🌶️🌶️ Tangy & Peppery
⏱️ Cook Time: 45 mins Southern Heritage

Sour Goraka Tuna (Fish Ambul Thiyal)

මාළු ඇඹුල් තියල් (Ambul Thiyal)

Ancient dry fish curry originated by coastal fishermen to preserve fresh tuna. Cubes of tuna coated in thick black goraka paste, black pepper, and simmered dry.

Key Ingredients:
Yellowfin Tuna Goraka Paste Heavy Black Pepper Curry Leaves & Pandan
🍯 Naturally Sweet & Creamy
⏱️ Prep Time: 5 mins Classic Village Dessert

Buffalo Curd with Wild Kithul Treacle

මී කිරි සහ කිතුල් පැණි (Curd & Treacle)

Creamy, rich water buffalo curd fermented in unglazed earthenware pots, drenched in pure liquid amber sap harvested from wild highland Kithul palm flowers.

Key Ingredients:
Clay Pot Buffalo Curd Pure Rainforest Kithul Treacle Fresh Grated Nutmeg
