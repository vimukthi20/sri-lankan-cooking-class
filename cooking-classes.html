Traditional Village Rice & Curry Masterclass

The flagship Sri Lankan culinary experience. Master the balance of coconut milk, tamarind, and roasted spices while cooking in authentic clay pots over wood fires.

  • Cook 6 curries: Chicken/Fish, Dhal, Polos, Brinjal Moju, Beetroot & Pumpkin

Night Street Food Experience

Hoppers, Rotti & Street Food Evening Safari

Learn how Sri Lankans feast when the sun sets. Master the round wok wrist-swirl for crispy bowl hoppers, knead flaky coconut rotti, and temper spicy street gravies.

  • Master crispy egg hoppers (*biththara appa*) & sweet milk hoppers
  • Make warm Pol Rotti on terracotta griddles with chili lunu miris
  • Cook caramelized onion Seeni Sambol & aromatic chicken street curry
  • Twilight candlelit garden dining with local Ceylon arrack punch
